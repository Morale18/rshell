So how do tokens works?
This is needed to understand what we are doing in the shell

we start with char *tokens and char **tokens
Lets get these two out of the way
When we do char *token we are pointing to
a char/start of a string

When we do char **tokens we are pointing to
an array of strings

Summary: 
    char *token: a single string
    char **tokens: a list of strings

Now What are tokens?
Lets say we have "ls -l"
This combination of chars make up 2 tokens.
token 1: ls
token 2: -l

But what about the space???? GREAT QUESTION

You're right to believe the space would cause an issue.
Hence, why we use strtok()
strtok() breaks a string into pieces known as "tokens" 
seperate by delimeter characters
if we have strtok(input, " "); our delimeter is " " or space
so whenever it sees a space it cuts it with '\0'
